About Maison du Regard Bleu

Art, atmosphere, and a point of view.

Maison du Regard Bleu presents original work by San Antonio artist Jen Jelinski. Her paintings move between expressive portraiture, abstraction, and geometric form, often paired with traditional frames and interiors that create tension between old-world elegance and modern expression.

Jen is currently focused on finished original works and fine art prints rather than commissioned pieces.
