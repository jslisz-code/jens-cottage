About the Artist

Structure, instinct, and a point of view.

Deeply influenced by Picasso’s Cubist period, her work explores angular, structured forms that draw out subconscious feelings of self-awareness and introspection. Her instinctive palette of blues and greens reflects the Mediterranean landscape of her upbringing, while recurring references to the evil eye introduce themes of protection, perception, and inner consciousness. Throughout her work, vulnerability is balanced with sophistication, creating compositions that feel both emotionally exposed and deliberately composed.

Her paintings are created to live beyond the canvas, bringing character, depth, and conversation into the spaces they inhabit. Each work is intended to stand on its own while complementing thoughtfully designed residential, hospitality, and gallery environments.

Jen is currently focused on finished original works and fine art prints rather than commissioned pieces.
