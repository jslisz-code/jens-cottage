About Jen's Cottage

Art, antiques, and rooms with a point of view.

Jen's Cottage is built around expressive work with personality. The paintings move between portrait, abstraction, and geometric form, often paired with traditional frames and interiors that create tension between old-world elegance and modern expression.

That contrast is the signature: bold work, beautifully staged.

Collected slowly. Chosen instinctively.
